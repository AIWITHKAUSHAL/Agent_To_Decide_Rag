# Classroom Store Refund Policy

This is fictional teaching material, not a real company's policy.

## Eligibility
Unused physical products returned within 30 calendar days of delivery are eligible for a refund. A receipt or order number is required. Used products and returns after 30 days are not eligible.

## Refund calculation
For an eligible return, refund 90% of the product purchase price. The remaining 10% is a restocking fee. Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Do not subtract shipping from the product price if it is already listed separately. For multiple eligible products, apply 90% to the sum of their product prices.

## Damaged products
Products reported damaged on delivery within 7 days qualify for a full product-price refund, with no restocking fee. Original shipping charges remain non-refundable.

## Processing
Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.

## Digital products
Downloaded digital products are non-refundable. An undownloaded digital product can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase.

## Support
Contact classroom-support@example.com with your order number to request a return.
